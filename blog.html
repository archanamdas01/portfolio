Business analytics

How better data tracking improves business decisions

When business information is spread across files, emails, and individual teams, tracking work becomes slow and difficult. A centralized system gives stakeholders a clearer view of progress, pending work, and the information needed for decisions.

My Lead Tracking System project explored this idea by organizing BQ, RFP, Contract, and STPO workflow data in one place. Features such as role-based access, search, CRUD operations, and Excel reporting can turn routine record management into a more reliable source of business insight.

AI & e-commerce

Semantic search: moving beyond keywords

Traditional product search relies heavily on exact keywords. This can make it difficult for customers to find what they want when they describe an idea, style, or need in natural language.

My ongoing research on LLM-based semantic search for fashion e-commerce compares keyword search with LLM-based approaches. The goal is to understand whether semantic search can improve discovery, relevance, and the overall user experience—and how those results can be evaluated through user feedback and statistical analysis.

Career journey

Why I chose business analytics after engineering

Computer Science Engineering taught me how to think systematically and build technical solutions. Business Analytics adds another important perspective: understanding the problem, the people involved, and the decisions that data can support.

Through my MBA, I am developing the ability to connect technical tools such as SQL, Excel, and web development with real business workflows. I enjoy working in that space between data, technology, and practical impact.
